📖 Definitions

⚡ Voltage (V)
Electrical pressure that pushes charge through a circuit. Measured in Volts (V).
🔌 Current (I)
Flow of electric charge. Measured in Amperes (A).
🛡️ Resistance (R)
Opposition to current flow. Measured in Ohms (Ω).
📐 Ohm's Law
V = I × R — Voltage equals Current multiplied by Resistance.
🔋 Series Circuit
Single path for current. Same current flows through all components.
⚡ Parallel Circuit
Multiple paths. Same voltage across each branch. Current adds up.
